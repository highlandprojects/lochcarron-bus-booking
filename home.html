Applecross and Lochcarron to Inverness

For the garage

The office sign in

Somebody has rung up and wants a seat. Take the booking, see the day and how many seats are left, change or cancel a booking, and get the list of everyone who has to be telephoned if a run is stood down.

 The driver sign in

The run in road order on a phone. Tap people on, record anybody who flags the bus down, and tidy up at the end. Works with no signal — everything is kept on the phone and sends itself later.

For customers

Book a seat online anyone

What a customer sees. This is the page to send people to, or to have open at the counter if somebody would rather book it themselves.

 The route and every stop anyone

Both directions on a map, every stop in order with its time. Useful on the telephone when somebody asks where the bus actually goes.
The two garage screens use your normal Ross Group staff login — the same email address and password as tyres, roadside and the rest. There is no separate bus password to remember, and nobody has to be set up twice. Whether a particular person can open the bus is decided on the same who can do what screen as everything else.
